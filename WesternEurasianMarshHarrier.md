# Western (Eurasian) marsh harrier

## Circus aeruginosus (LA) / Bruine kiekendief (NL) / Болотный лунь (RU)

![Western (Eurasian) marsh harrier](https://images.birdfact.com/production/marsh-harrier_2023-03-06-105509_dksk.jpg?w=1800&h=1350&q=80&auto=format&fit=crop&dm=1678100109&s=87ee4c6326d4f2fa6fcfbd14718eb35e)

### Description

This species has a wide breeding range from Europe and northwestern Africa to Central Asia and the northern parts of the Middle East. It breeds in almost every country of Europe but is absent from mountainous regions and subarctic Scandinavia. The Western marsh harrier has a population of 500.000–2.000.000.

It is strongly associated with wetland areas, especially those rich in common reed. It can also be met with in a variety of other open habitats, such as farmland and grassland, particularly where these border marshland.

It is a large harrier. The western marsh harrier is 48-56 cm in length, has a wingspan of 115-130 cm, and a weight of 400-650 g in males and 550-800 g in females.

The male's plumage is mostly a cryptic reddish-brown with lighter yellowish streaks, which are particularly prominent on the breast. The head and shoulders are mostly pale greyish-yellowish. The female is almost entirely chocolate-brown.
